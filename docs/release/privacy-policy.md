# Gut Elements app — Privacy Policy

> Published at **https://gutelements.com/app-privacy**. The app's Settings screen and the Play listing link to that URL; keep this copy in sync with the live page.

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026
**Publisher:** Gut Elements (gutelements.com)
**Contact:** hello@gutelements.com

## Summary
The Gut Elements app keeps your bowel movement logs on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share them. The app has no account system and does not connect to the internet.

## What the app stores
When you use the app, it saves the following **only on your device**:
- Each bowel movement you log: the Bristol Stool Scale type (1–7) and the date and time.
- When each entry was created and last edited.
- Whether you have completed the introduction screens.

## What we collect
**None of the above leaves your device.** The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data to us or anyone else. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party services in the app.

## Backups and device transfers
The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your logs are not copied to Google Drive or a new phone. This also means that if you uninstall the app, change phones or clear the app's data, your logs are permanently removed.

## Your control
- Edit or delete any entry at any time.
- Delete all of your data in **Settings → Delete all local data**.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, recover or export it for you.

## Links
The app links to gutelements.com and this policy. Those pages open in your browser and are covered by the website's own privacy policy.

## Health information
Gut Elements is for personal tracking and education. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children — or from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a future version of the app begins collecting any data (for example, optional cloud backup), we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app before anything is collected.
