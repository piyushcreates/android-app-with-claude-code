## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a restorative, evidence-aware digital environment for personal digestive wellness. The design blends warm minimal wellness with scientific clarity ("clinical-lite"). It rejects both childish, stigmatizing poop emojis and cold, fluorescent hospital aesthetics.

The visual tone is respectful, calm, and grounded:
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: Built on natural linen, oatmeal, and unbleached fiber tones rather than stark clinical whites, reducing medical anxiety.
- **Scientific Dignity**: The Bristol Stool Scale is treated with botanical and anatomical precision—relying on geometric abstraction, refined micro-illustrations, and tonal chromatic coding.
- **Empathetic Minimalism**: Generous negative space, whisper-soft boundaries, and tactile micro-interactions reassure users during daily health logging.

## Colors

The palette balances restorative sage botanicals with warm domestic neutrals and non-judgmental categorical indicators:

- **Primary (`#3E5C49`)**: Deep Sage Green. Evokes gut microbiome vitality, balance, and scientific grounding. Used for primary actions, active navigational states, and optimal metrics.
- **Secondary (`#C45834`)**: Terracotta Clay. Drawn from the core brand profile; applied to low-frequency alerts, firm-texture indicators, and focused emphasis without alarmist red connotations.
- **Tertiary (`#D49438`)**: Warm Ochre/Amber. Represents transitional stool consistencies, warnings, and metabolic circadian insights.
- **Neutral (`#767069`)**: Grounded Warm Gray. Controls secondary interface borders, icons, and muted typographic roles.
- **Bristol Scale Chromatic Mapping**:
  - *Hard / Type 1–2*: Soft Terracotta (`#B25E42`) paired with pale clay backgrounds (`#FBEFEA`).
  - *Optimal / Type 3–4*: Gentle Olive & Deep Sage (`#466B53`) paired with soft matcha tint (`#EEF3EF`).
  - *Loose / Type 5–7*: Warm Amber & Ochre (`#C6853A`) paired with honey-tinted warmth (`#FBF4E8`).
- **Canvas & Textures**: Core background is unbleached linen (`#F4F0E7`), topped with clean card elevations (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure crisp legibility for complex digestive logs. Primary reading text is soft dark charcoal (`#261C24`), preventing harsh ocular contrast.

## Typography

The type system blends contemporary scientific clarity with quiet humanism:

- **Headlines (Manrope)**: Geometric yet warm, Manrope provides clinical credibility without institutional chill. Headings use medium and semibold weights with subtle negative tracking for a composed, high-end journal aesthetic.
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter)**: High legibility at small sizes ensures that detailed health questionnaires, timestamp logs, and transit-time calculations remain effortlessly readable.
- **Numeric & Metric Treatments**: Digits inside hydration, time stamps, and Bristol scales feature tabular alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain orderly data review.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first lifestyle health interface with a strict 3-destination core: **Today**, **History**, and **Insights**.

- **Rhythm & Grid**:
  - **Mobile (Base to 600px)**: 4-column layout, fluid containers with a strict 16px lateral margin. Maximizes single-thumb logging reach.
  - **Tablet (601px to 1024px)**: 8-column layout centered with max width 720px for journaling flows.
  - **Desktop (1025px+)**: 12-column layout with an ergonomic central reading shell locked at 960px.
- **Spatial Generosity**: The gut health journey uses unhurried, generous vertical spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl` between modular cards) to prevent cognitive overload during acute symptom episodes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through warm tonal layering and soft ambient contact shadows rather than high-contrast drops:

- **Layering Model**:
  - *Base Layer*: Canvas Oatmeal (`#F4F0E7`).
  - *Recessed Surface*: Inset containers or non-active selectors use Linen (`#ECE6D8`).
  - *Resting Cards*: Pure elevated white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E2DACB`).
  - *Active / Popovers*: Warm ambient diffuse shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(38, 28, 36, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 28, 36, 0.03)`).
- **Physical Demarcation**: Elevational movement is restrained. Cards do not hover dramatically; instead, selections respond with subtle inward rim rings and serene tint changes.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature without feeling juvenile:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 2`)**: Buttons, badges, and segmented toggles utilize `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner rounding.
- **Large Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`)**: Cards, Bristol selector tiles, and modal bottom sheets apply generous `1rem` to `1.5rem` radiuses, evoking natural, polished pebble forms.
- **Pill Geometry**: Reserved strictly for high-frequency micro-status tags, transit time badges, and the floating 3-tab navigation bar.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Sage (`#3E5C49`) with pure white text. Padding `14px 24px`, roundedness `1rem`. Active state settles into deep forest tone.
- **Secondary**: Elevated White surface with a 1px border (`#E2DACB`) and dark charcoal text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background with muted charcoal typography; displays a subtle `#ECE6D8` wash on hover.

### Bristol Stool Scale Selector Tiles
- Standardized 7-item interactive modules.
- **Visuals**: Abstract, scientifically accurate cross-section icons (no emoji humor).
- **Idle State**: White surface with a faint `#E2DACB` outline, neutral charcoal typography, and an archetype pill label.
- **Selected State**: Outlined by a 2px rim tinted according to consistency group (Terracotta for Types 1–2, Deep Sage for Types 3–4, Amber for Types 5–7), accompanied by a translucent 8% chromatic background tint.

### Navigation (The Triad Tab Bar)
- Max 3 tabs: **Today**, **History**, **Insights**.
- Executed as a floating pill bar docked above the mobile safe area.
- Background: Blended semi-translucent linen (`rgba(244, 240, 231, 0.92)`) with a `backdrop-blur(12px)` and hairline outline.
- Active item displays an organic sage dot indicator with semibold weight transitions.

### Cards & Modular Trackers
- Containers default to white backgrounds with `1.25rem` radius and `1.25rem` inner padding.
- Symptom tags (bloating, cramp, urgency) sit as selectable toggle chips within sub-panels, utilizing neutral linen when off and deep sage when engaged.

### Form Inputs & Slider Controls
- Inputs feature warm oatmeal fill, `1px solid #E2DACB` borders, and charcoal text. Focus shifts border to `#3E5C49` with an ultra-light sage outer aura (`0 0 0 3px rgba(62, 92, 73, 0.15)`).
- Urgency and pain scales use continuous smooth sliders filled with an earthy gradient ranging from muted sage to soft terracotta.
